Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Rclone backup is a personal tool used only by its owner, Miloš Vích, to back up his own files.

What data the app accesses

The app requests the Google Drive scope drive.file. With it, the app can only create, read and delete files that it created itself. It cannot see any other files in Google Drive.

How the data is used

The app uploads encrypted backup data to the owner's Google Drive and reads it back to restore files. The data is encrypted on the owner's computer before upload.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone. No analytics, advertising or tracking is used. The app has no server; it runs only on the owner's computer.

Storage and deletion

OAuth tokens are stored only in a local configuration file on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and backup data can be deleted from Google Drive.
